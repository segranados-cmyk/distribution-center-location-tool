Model Transparency
  • Data: Saved snapshot of U.S. Census ACS 2024 5-Year state-level estimates; they may lag current conditions.
  • Median earnings approximate labor cost; they are not distribution-center wages, real estate costs, or total operating expenses.
  • Workforce is the share of residents aged 18–64, not a measure of job applicants or logistics skills.
  • Rankings are relative to the selected states and support initial screening; they do not guarantee site success.

Source: U.S. Census ACS 2024 5-Year · Saved 2024 ACS snapshot · 50 states, excluding Washington, D.C. and territories · Missing or invalid values are excluded.
